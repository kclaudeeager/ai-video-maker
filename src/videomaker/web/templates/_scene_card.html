Context: a `QuotaView`. `storyboard.html` imports the first two (`{% from "_scene_card.html" import ... %}`). A reply from `choose`, `motion` or `search` renders this template *directly* instead, and the body at the bottom is then the updated card followed by the gate card as an out-of-band swap — guarded, because Jinja builds an imported module by running that body too. `quota_card` is drawn *inside* `gate_card` rather than beside it.
